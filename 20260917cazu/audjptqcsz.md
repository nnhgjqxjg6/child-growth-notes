# ART center of Northwest Women’s and Children’s Hospital

> 更新时间：2026-09-17 (UTC+8)

The ART center of Northwest Women’s and Children’s Hospital was established in 2001.

At present, we can do：

**IVF/ICSI-ET**(in vitro fertilization/intracytoplasmic sperm injection-embryo transfer)

**TESE** (testicular sperm extraction)

**Micro-TES****E** (microdissection testicular sperm extraction)

**PGS**(preimplantation genetic screening)

**PGD** (preimplantation genetic diagnosis)

**IVM** (in vitro immature)

**AIH** (artificial insemination with husband sperm)

**AID** (artificial insemination by donor)

**Embryo freeze**

**Oocyte freeze**

**Sperm freeze**

and **multifetal pregnancy reduction**.

Our diagnosis and treatment projects include male and female infertility, recurrent spontaneous abortion, recurrent implantation failure and reproductive endocrine diseases.

In August 2011, our center was approved as **the only human sperm bank in Shaanxi Province.** By the end of May 2018, 27551 semen samples had been stored in our center, which had been supplied to 19 reproductive centers in 12 provinces.

In 2012, our center took the lead in carrying out **Micro-TESE** in Northwest China to help azoospermia patients. We had completed 535 cases of Micro-TESE surgery and at the domestic leading level.
In 2015, our center obtained **ISO9001** quality management system certification. In 2018, we passed the iso9001:2015 revision and upgrading certification.

In 2019, our center received 390000 outpatients and we had completed 16177 IVF cycles (9586 fresh cycles and 6591 frozen cycles); Our **clinical pregnancy rate per cycle was 60%**. By the end of November 2019, a total of 34753 IVF babies were born in our center (18269 boys and 16484 girls).

Besides the mainland of China, our center has also provided medical services for the foreign infertility couples from many other countries, including the United States, Britain, Kenya and Pakistan. **We sincerely welcome all the infertility patients over the world to take reproduction medical treatments at our center.**
